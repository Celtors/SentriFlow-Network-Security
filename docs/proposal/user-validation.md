# Prepared user-validation kit

No participants have been contacted and no responses have been collected. Tracker goals are 30+ survey responses and 3–5 interviews; those are targets only.

Before publishing, state who can access responses, an actual deletion date and a team contact. Participation must be voluntary and avoid names, credentials, actual payloads and employer-confidential information. Do not call a form anonymous if the chosen platform collects identifiable accounts. Use only synthetic traffic in observed tasks.

Suggested ten survey questions:

1. Main laptop use: student, remote worker, SME staff, other.
2. Frequency of uncertainty about outbound application traffic: never to very often.
3. Whether the respondent investigated an unexpected connection in the last month.
4. Confidence identifying the application and destination, 1–5.
5. Network/privacy tools already used, allowing multiple selections.
6. Main investigation barriers: terminology, application identification, encryption, blocking decisions, other.
7. Usefulness of a process/host/masked-pattern prompt, 1–5.
8. Preferred unanswered action: Block, Allow, unsure; optional explanation.
9. Willingness to trust an explicitly explained local CA before/after explanation; record the two answers separately.
10. Main deterrent: too many prompts, broken sites, setup, trust, performance, other.

Interview tasks: explain a dummy Halt prompt; choose once vs remembered decision; explain its future scope; observe timeout; review CA consent/removal; identify confusing wording and unacceptable disruption. Record anonymous ID, consent, task success/time and permission for exact anonymous quotes. Separate observation from interpretation.

Report actual valid-response denominators, missing responses, recruitment method, dates and limitations. Integrate real aggregate results into Background/Opportunity and use only permissioned quotes. Keep raw participant data in access-controlled private storage, not Git. If evidence contradicts assumptions, revise the proposal rather than inventing validation.
